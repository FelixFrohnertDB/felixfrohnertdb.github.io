 During my mentorship program, we developed a metric to quantify information flow in parameterized quantum circuits, which involved creating a graph-based representation of quantum circuits and introducing a distance metric utilizing mutual information between gate nodes. Building on this, we explored path-based optimization methods for variational quantum algorithms, demonstrating improvements in convergence for tasks like computing ground state energies and binary classification. This project led to a collaboration, resulting in a publication co-authored with researchers from the University of Toronto and Harvard University.
